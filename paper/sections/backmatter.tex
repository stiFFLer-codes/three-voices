\subsection*{Funding}

\subsection*{Competing Interests}

The authors have no relevant financial or non-financial interests to

\subsection*{Ethics Approval}

This study involved no human participants and no animals. It is a secondary
analysis of the UCI Maternal Health Risk dataset, which is public, released
under CC BY 4.0, and contains no identifiable personal data. Ethics approval
was therefore not required and none was sought. The studies with mothers,
community health workers and clinicians that this paper names as its next
step are gated on ethics clearance that has not been obtained and is not
claimed anywhere in this work.

\subsection*{Consent to Participate and Consent to Publish}

Not applicable. No human participants were recruited and no individual
person's data is reported.

\subsection*{Data and Code Availability}

We use the UCI Maternal Health Risk dataset~\citep{ahmed2020maternal}
(repository id 863),
public and released under CC BY 4.0, at
\url{https://doi.org/10.24432/C5DP5D}. No other data is used and no real
patient record appears in this work.

All code, the rendered artifacts for all four cases across all three tiers,
the cross-validation and SHAP tables, and the evaluation matrix are archived
at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22252076}. That is the concept DOI
and resolves to the latest release; development history is at
\url{https://github.com/stiFFLer-codes/three-voices}. Every figure and table
in this paper regenerates from the five commands listed in
Section~\ref{sec:repro}.

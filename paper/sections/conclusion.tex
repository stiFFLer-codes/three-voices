\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a rendering pipeline that takes one maternal-health risk prediction
and issues three different assertions from it, one per reader, withholding the
highest-severity signal from the two channels that cannot interrogate the
model whenever the top-two class probabilities fall inside a fixed margin.
Every string in it is a template filled from the case's SHAP attributions and
no language model runs at inference, so any rendering here can be regenerated
and checked line by line. We also re-benchmarked the dataset underneath it:
561 of the 1012 usable rows are exact duplicates, retaining them across the
split moves macro-recall from $0.580$ to $0.859$, and $0.580$ is the figure
this vehicle earns once they are dropped.

Row 382 is the case that shows what the rule is for. The model calls a
true-low record high risk on a top-two margin of $0.002$, and the mother
receives amber where the argmax alone would have sent red. That is still a
false referral. The gate made the error smaller, not correct. Whether any of
this helps a mother, a health worker or a clinician is untested here and gated
on the ethics clearance Section~\ref{sec:limitations} describes.

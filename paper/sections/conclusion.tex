\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a rendering pipeline that takes one maternal-health risk prediction
and issues three different assertions from it, one per reader, withholding the
highest-severity signal from the two channels that cannot interrogate the
model whenever the top-two class probabilities fall inside a fixed margin.
All text comes from fixed templates, selected by the band and filled from the
SHAP attributions, with no language model at inference, so any rendering here
can be regenerated and checked line by line. We also re-benchmarked the dataset underneath it:
561 of the 1012 usable rows are exact duplicates, retaining them across the
split moves macro-recall from $0.580$ to $0.859$, and $0.580$ is the figure
this vehicle earns once they are dropped.

Row 325 is the case that shows what the rule is for. Out of fold, the model
calls this true-low record high risk on a top-two margin of $0.029$, and the
mother receives amber where the argmax alone would have sent red. That is still
a false referral. The gate made the error smaller, not correct. Across all 451
records, the gate derates 11 of the 114 high-risk calls. Three of those 11 are
truly high-risk, against 78 of the 103 calls it keeps as \textsc{red}. Whether any of this helps a mother, a health worker or a
clinician is untested here and gated on the ethics clearance
Section~\ref{sec:limitations} describes.
